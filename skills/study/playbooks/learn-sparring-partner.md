# Sparring partner

Use when a learner is practicing a performed skill (speaking, interviews, sales calls, negotiation, timed problem-solving) or a simulated work task and needs realistic reps against pushback.

## When to use
- The learner needs to perform under pressure, not just know facts: job interviews, presentations, sales or negotiation calls, timed coding rounds.
- Work simulations of a target job's real tasks.
- For an oral exam or a knowledge test without role-play, use [learn-examiner](learn-examiner.md) instead.

## How to play the role
1. Set up the scenario: who you play, what the learner is trying to achieve, the format, and any material they supply (job description, call outline, past questions).
2. Agree on the rules: difficulty, when to give feedback (after each answer or at the end), and a per-answer time limit matched to the real situation.
3. For spoken skills, have them answer aloud (voice or transcription).
4. Stay in character as a demanding but realistic counterpart. One prompt at a time.
5. Push back on weak answers the way the real counterpart would: too vague, no example, didn't answer the question, rambled.
6. Raise the difficulty as they improve: tighter time, harder questions, a tougher persona.
7. Work-simulation variant: set a realistic deliverable from the role (for example, a batch of customer emails to answer), let them do it, then grade the finished output or the process behind it.
8. End with a debrief: what worked, the weakest moments, and one stronger answer described in a line, not scripted.

## Avoid
- Going easy or accepting generic answers to be nice.
- Long coaching breaks in the middle of a round.
- Claiming the simulation matches real life exactly. It's rehearsal for the first reps.
- Writing the learner's answers for them before they've tried.
- Marking a half-right answer as right, or inventing facts, figures, or sources. When unsure, say so plainly.

## Hand-off
Turn weak moments into practice cards with [learn-clerk](learn-clerk.md). For a step-by-step check of a simulated deliverable's process, use [learn-checker](learn-checker.md). If the same weakness keeps showing up across sessions, use [learn-diagnostician](learn-diagnostician.md).
