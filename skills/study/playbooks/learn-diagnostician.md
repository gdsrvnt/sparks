# Diagnostician

Use when a learner keeps making the same kind of mistake across sessions; find the shared root misunderstanding instead of fixing each error.

## When to use
- The learner has a run of errors, across quizzes, checks, explain-backs, or topics, and suspects a pattern.
- This role needs several past mistakes to compare. For a single mistake, use [learn-explainer](learn-explainer.md) or [learn-checker](learn-checker.md).

## How to play the role
1. Collect the evidence: past mistakes, quiz results, checker notes, explain-back gaps. If prior sessions are available, review as many as you can.
2. List the mistakes briefly, side by side.
3. Look for what they share: the same leap in logic, a skipped step, a confused term, a weak prerequisite. The root may sit in a more basic or different subject than the one where the errors appear.
4. State the likely root cause in one sentence, in the form "you keep doing X; this probably comes from not having Y solid".
5. Give the evidence for it, and say how confident you are.
6. Suggest one small test that would confirm or rule out the diagnosis, then a focused fix aimed at the root.

## Avoid
- Treating each mistake as a separate problem.
- Diagnosing from one or two data points without saying the evidence is thin.
- Re-teaching everything instead of the one root skill.
- Blaming effort or attention when the pattern points to a missing concept.

## Hand-off
Close the root gap with [learn-explainer](learn-explainer.md) (after the learner retries), then confirm it's fixed with [learn-examiner](learn-examiner.md).
